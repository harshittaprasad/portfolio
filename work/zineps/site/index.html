      What exactly is Zineps?

      Zineps is the intelligent layer between e-commerce and logistics. Online stores automate shipping from order to return, and carriers manage their customers through the partner panel. One platform, one dataset.

      Who is it for?

      Both sides of the shipment. Online stores and wholesalers use Zineps to cut shipping costs and manual work. Carriers, forwarders and 3PLs publish their services and manage everything centrally.

      Do I need a shipping contract already?

      No. You can connect your own contracts, use the rates of our affiliated partners, or run both side by side and let the engine pick per shipment.

      Which systems does Zineps integrate with?

      Shopify, WooCommerce, Bol.com, Amazon, Exact, Lightspeed and many more, plus an open API for custom integrations and WMS or ERP connections.

      What does it cost?

      A transparent SaaS subscription with optional costs per shipment. Packages differ for merchants and logistics partners, with no hidden surcharges.

      How quickly can I start?

      Within minutes. Connect your shop or register as a partner and ship the same day. Most merchants print their first label in under an hour.
