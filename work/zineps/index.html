ProblemStatement

Zineps does a lot, for two very different audiences , and the website has one scroll to make all of it feel clear, credible and easy to start with.

AboutProject

Zineps helps online stores and wholesalers automate shipping, from the order to the return, and gives carriers, forwarders and 3PLs a place to publish their services and manage their customers. One platform and one set of data, with two very different people looking at it.

I redesigned the website around one question: how do you make a product this broad feel clear, credible and easy to start with, in a single scroll?

PainPoints

UserPain Points

  1. 01
    Two audiences

    Merchants want lower shipping costs and less manual work. Logistics partners want to sell their services and keep their customers. Both land on the same page.

  2. 02
    A wide product

    Orders, labels, rates, tracking, returns, analytics and an AI layer, each worth a page of its own.

  3. 03
    An abstract promise

    “Partner rates” and “shipping AI” are easy to claim and hard to picture.

Redesigned so a wide product feels simple, the page shows the product working before the copy has to explain it, and gives merchants and logistics partners a moment each.

01 Hero

Show the product, not a picture of it

The hero holds a working dashboard. Visitors switch between six views and search the shipments, so the product explains itself before the copy has to.

02 Proof

Proof straight after the promise

Three plain numbers say how big the network is, each with one line on what it means for a shop.

03 Audiences

Two audiences, one page

Parcels and freight sit side by side for merchants, and logistics providers get a section of their own, written to them.

04 Shipping AI

AI as a real decision

One lane, four carrier options, labelled recommended, fastest, cheapest or your own contract. Picking one shows the trade-off.

05 Platform

Everything at a glance

Features as small, working visuals rather than icons and bullet points: a cost trend, a map of destinations, integrations, uptime and address checks.

06 Start

Make starting feel small

Connect, match and ship, each with how long it takes, then the questions people ask before they commit.

A working redesign,not a mockup.

A complete, responsive page built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Every view in the hero dashboard works, the menus open and close from the keyboard, the theme can be switched, and motion is toned down for anyone whose device asks for less.
