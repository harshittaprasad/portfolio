ProblemStatement

People struggle to see where their money goes, because a balance and a list of payments say what happened, not whether they’re on track.

AboutProject

FlowLedger connects to your bank once, then records and categorises every transaction for you. On top of that ledger it adds what a spreadsheet can’t: a forecast towards your goals, alerts when spending drifts, a shared tab with friends, and a monthly report written in plain language.

It’s designed phone-first, for the moments people actually check their money: in a queue, after dinner, just before buying something.

PainPoints

UserPain Points

  1. 01
    Numbers without meaning

    A balance and a list of payments say what happened, not whether you’re on track.

  2. 02
    Money is social

    Dinners, trips and rent get shared, and keeping score with friends is awkward.

  3. 03
    Small leaks add up

    Subscriptions and recurring bills are easy to forget until they’re charged.

  4. 04
    It’s personal

    People check their finances in public, so the app has to be discreet.

Designed to make money feel calm, FlowLedger records every payment on its own, explains your month in plain language, and keeps the maths between friends out of the way.
