I’ve always been a little too curious for my own good. I like asking why things work the way they do, noticing the details people usually skip over, and occasionally taking the long way around just to see where it goes. Now, I do that through UX — part research, part problem-solving, part making things feel a little more obvious than they did before. I’m drawn to human behaviour, thoughtful interactions, and the tiny decisions that can make an experience click. Somewhere between the kid who was endlessly curious and the designer I’m becoming, I’m still figuring things out — just with better fonts now.
